Gensler, Community Civic Project

As a User Experience Designer for Gensler's Community Civic Project, I contributed to designing spaces that enhance community engagement and civic participation. This project focused on creating accessible, inclusive environments that serve diverse community needs.

Problem

Many civic spaces fail to adequately serve all community members, with issues of accessibility, inclusivity, and functionality limiting engagement and participation. Traditional designs often don't account for diverse needs or changing community dynamics.

Goal

To design user-centered civic spaces that foster community connection, increase engagement, and provide accessible services for all residents regardless of age, ability, or background.

Design Approach

  • Human-centered design process focusing on community needs and feedback
  • Accessible and inclusive spatial layouts that accommodate diverse users
  • Integration of technology to enhance civic services and community engagement

Key Solutions

  • Flexible, multi-purpose spaces that adapt to different community needs
  • Sustainable design elements that reduce environmental impact
  • Intuitive wayfinding systems to improve navigation and accessibility

User Research

Conducted extensive community surveys, interviews, and observational studies to understand diverse needs and usage patterns.

Collaborative Design

Facilitated design workshops with community stakeholders to ensure solutions reflected actual community priorities.

Implementation Strategy

Developed phased implementation plans to minimize disruption while maximizing community benefit throughout the project.

Project Outcomes

Community Engagement Increase

Impact Assessment

Post-implementation surveys showed a 45% increase in community participation and usage of the redesigned civic spaces.

Accessibility Improvements

Design Achievement

The project received recognition for exceeding ADA requirements and implementing innovative accessibility features throughout the space.

Sustainability Recognition

Environmental Impact

The design achieved LEED Gold certification through integration of sustainable materials, energy-efficient systems, and green space preservation.
